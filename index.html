About Us

Advocates You Can Rely On

Founded in Nakuru, J. A. Simiyu & Co. has grown into one of Kenya's most respected law firms. Our team of seasoned advocates brings decades of combined experience across commercial, civil, and criminal law.

We are driven by a simple philosophy: every client deserves passionate representation and clear, honest advice. From high-stakes corporate litigation to personal legal matters, we stand with you every step of the way.
